Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our laundry memberships, scheduled pickups, and garment care.

When you select a membership plan, you receive dedicated WashAura laundry hampers and can immediately schedule your first pickup. Every week or twice a month, our courier collects your garments and returns them clean, pressed, and crisply folded within 24 to 48 hours.
Yes, absolutely! There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. You can pause your deliveries when traveling, roll over unused poundage to the next billing cycle, or cancel directly from your Member Dashboard with a single click.
WashAura exclusively utilizes non-toxic, plant-based biodegradable detergents and closed-loop hydrocarbon/silicone dry cleaning technology. We never use PERC, chlorine, or synthetic dyes, ensuring your garments remain skin-safe and pristine.
Yes! Inside your Member Dashboard, you can specify water temperature (cold/warm), hypoallergenic unscented wash, fabric softener preferences, starch levels for collared shirts (none, light, medium), and hanger vs. folded delivery.
Standard Wash & Fold is returned in 24 to 48 hours. Dry cleaning and delicate garment care require 48 hours for optimal stain release and hand finishing. Executive and VIP members also have access to Same-Day and Overnight Express turnaround.
Delicate fabrics undergo individual fiber analysis before processing. Hand-beaded gowns, pure cashmere, and silks receive our Luxury Valet treatment: hand-spotting, low-temperature organic immersion, air drying on shaped forms, and tension-free artisanal steam pressing.
