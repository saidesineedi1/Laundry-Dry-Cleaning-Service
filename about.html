Our Origin Story

From an Artisan Workshop to Your Daily Wardrobe

WashAura began from a simple conviction: your most cherished garments deserve the same gentle, thoughtful care as your skin. Frustrated by harsh industrial chemicals, unpleasant petrochemical fumes, and careless mass-processing, our founders set out to create a higher standard of garment care.

We paired gentle plant-based detergents and closed-loop silicone dry cleaning with artisan steam finishing and punctual doorstep pickup. Today, our philosophy remains unchanged: every collared shirt, delicate silk dress, and set of sheets is treated with individualized fiber care and delivered fresh to your door.

Our 6 Core Commitments to You & Your Wardrobe

Botanical & Hypoallergenic
