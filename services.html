THE ATELIER PROTOCOL

Your Wardrobe, Our Promise

Every WashAura garment follows a master four-step protocol — from intake inspection to wardrobe-ready delivery — ensuring immaculate cleanliness and fiber preservation.

01

Inspection & Tagging

We begin with individual item barcode tagging, color fastness checks, and optical stain analysis. Every piece receives custom attention.

02

Eco-Solvent Purification

Our non-toxic closed-loop silicone solvent gently lifts deep dirt and persistent odors without abrasive fiber friction.

03

Artisan Steam Pressing

Italian vacuum steam tables relax fabric tension, eliminate wrinkles, and restore crisp factory drape to shirts and trousers.

04

Valet Presentation

Protected in breathable covers on contoured wooden hangers or neatly folded with crisp tissue wrap, ready for your closet.
