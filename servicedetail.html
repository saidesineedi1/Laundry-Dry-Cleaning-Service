THE TEXTILE SCIENCE

Crafted with Advanced
Eco-Solvent Care

At WashAura, we believe garment care is an artisanal science. Our atelier pairs German precision washing machinery with non-toxic, hypoallergenic solvents that cleanse deep into textile fibers without structural breakdown.

Each fabric category is cleaned under calibrated moisture, temperature, and agitation curves. Our low-temperature protocols preserve natural tensile strength — ensuring garments return with original texture and vibrant color integrity.

  • pH-balanced botanical detergents
  • Hydrocarbon non-toxic solvents
  • Zero abrasive chemical bleaches
  • Fabric longevity certified
THE ATELIER PROTOCOL

From Doorstep Pickup
To Wardrobe Return

Every order is treated as a bespoke assignment. Our couriers collect your garments in weather-sealed hampers, followed by 7-point atelier intake where stains, loose threads, and care tags are documented.

From Italian steam pressing to bespoke garment shaping, each piece receives custom finishing. You receive your garments crisply folded or hanging on cedar supports — perfectly fresh, sanitized, and immediately ready to wear.

Our 4-Stage Atelier Standard

The Science of Pristine Garments

Our 4-stage fabric care protocol ensures maximum cleanliness, hygiene, and garment longevity.

01

Barcode & Intake

Every piece is barcoded, examined under daylight lamps for stains, and categorized by fiber tolerance.

02

Purified Aqueous Wash

Washed in reverse-osmosis soft water with plant enzymes — zero aggressive bleach, zero fiber thinning.

03

Fiber-Conditioning Rinse

Calibrated at fabric neutral pH to release moisture without static cling, restoring softness and breathability.

04

Contour Steam & Pack

Finished on Italian vacuum steam tables and sealed in breathable garment bags for dust-free delivery.
